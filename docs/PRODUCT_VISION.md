# Product vision

BICS is Benestudio’s reusable digital client-acquisition infrastructure for US full-service residential interior-design studios. It combines a premium portfolio experience with project-fit guidance, structured inquiries, CRM-ready lead handling, analytics, and campaign-page support.

The product serves two users: homeowners selecting a serious studio, and Benestudio teams launching and maintaining client sites. A new client should primarily require a configuration, assets, factual project/service data, integrations, and QA—not reimplementation of core UI.

Success is not a prettier template. It is a credible studio presence that converts appropriate attention into qualified project conversations, while preserving client ownership of their domain, CRM, email, scheduling, analytics, and ad accounts.

Initial demo brand: **Avery House Interiors**, a clearly fictional full-service residential studio in Dallas–Fort Worth. Demo and outreach-concept modes will never transmit leads, collect analytics, index content, or imply an affiliation.
